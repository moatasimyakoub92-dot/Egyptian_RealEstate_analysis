# 🇪🇬 Egyptian Real Estate Market — Insights & Business Recommendations

> **Data Source:** 18,349 sale listings + 18,917 rental listings (2026)
> **Coverage:** 12 governorates, 15+ major towns, 17 property types

---

## 📊 Section 1: Key Market Insights

### 1.1 Market Size & Geographic Concentration

| Metric | Sale Market | Rent Market |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Total Listings | 18,349 | 18,917 |
| Median Price | 9.80M EGP | 65,000 EGP/month |
| IQR Range | 5.70M – 18.00M EGP | 40,000 – 100,000 EGP/mo |
| Price Skewness | 16.02 (extreme right skew) | 61.51 (extreme right skew) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Greater Cairo dominance:** Cairo + Giza together account for **80.3% of all sale listings** and **98.4% of all rental listings**. The Egyptian real estate market is essentially a Greater Cairo market with satellite resort destinations.

**Geographic breakdown (Sale):**
- **Cairo:** 9,315 (50.8%) — the clear heavyweight
- **Giza:** 5,417 (29.5%) — driven by Sheikh Zayed & 6 October satellite cities
- **North Coast:** 1,480 (8.1%) — seasonal/resort market
- **Red Sea (Hurghada):** 1,290 (7.0%) — tourism-driven
- All other governorates combined: < 5%

**Geographic breakdown (Rent):**
- **Cairo:** 15,195 (80.3%) — massive dominance
- **Giza:** 3,428 (18.1%)
- **Alexandria, North Coast, and others:** < 2% combined

> [!NOTE]
> The rental market is even more Cairo-concentrated than the sale market. Cities like Red Sea and Suez have almost no rental inventory despite active sale markets — signaling investor-driven (not resident-driven) demand in those regions.

---

### 1.2 Property Type Mix

**Sale market** is led by:
1. **Apartments** — 52.4% (9,611 listings, median 6.68M EGP)
2. **Villas** — 18.2% (3,339 listings, median 27.40M EGP)
3. **Townhouses** — 8.1% (1,486 listings, median 16.69M EGP)
4. **Chalets** — 7.7% (1,408 listings, median 9.10M EGP)

**Rent market** is even more apartment-heavy:
1. **Apartments** — 73.8% (13,954 listings, median 55,000 EGP/mo)
2. **Villas** — 10.3% (1,957 listings, median 166,000 EGP/mo)
3. **Duplexes** — 4.5%, **Townhouses** — 3.4%

> [!TIP]
> **Apartments are the backbone of both markets.** The 20% gap between apartment share in sale vs. rent (52% vs. 74%) suggests that apartments have proportionally higher tenant demand relative to supply, indicating strong rental potential for apartment investors.

### 1.4 Rental Yield Analysis

| City | Gross Annual Yield | Sale/SQM | Rent/SQM |
|------|:-----------------:|:--------:|:--------:|
| **North Coast** | **38.85%** | 96,235 | 3,116 |
| Red Sea | 8.06% | 74,400 | 500 |
| Cairo | 7.60% | 57,947 | 367 |
| Suez | 7.14% | 70,000 | 417 |
| Giza | 5.80% | 57,500 | 278 |
| Alexandria | 4.47% | 41,026 | 153 |
| Qalyubia | 2.27% | 22,703 | 43 |

> [!WARNING]
> **North Coast's 38.85% yield is misleading.** This extremely high figure is driven by short-term/seasonal rental pricing (575K EGP/month median), not year-round occupancy. The actual annualized yield with ~3 months of summer occupancy would be closer to **~10%**, which is still the strongest in Egypt.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Cairo offers the best reliable yield at 7.60%**, followed by Red Sea at 8.06%. Both benefit from year-round demand. Giza at 5.80% underperforms Cairo despite geographic proximity — likely due to newer, not-yet-mature communities with higher sale premiums but lower established rental demand.

**Overall market yield:** 7.96% (median rent 65K × 12 / median sale 9.8M)

## 💼 Section 2: Business Recommendations

### 🏗️ For Real Estate Developers

1. **Prioritize apartment development in Cairo's new satellite cities**
   - Apartments account for 52% of sales and 74% of rentals — the highest-demand segment
   - **Mostakbal City** (36,010 EGP/sqm) and **New Capital City** (42,901 EGP/sqm) offer the most affordable per-sqm entry points among Cairo-adjacent towns, with significant room for value appreciation as infrastructure matures
   - Target 150–200 sqm units (the market median) to hit the mass-market sweet spot

2. **Develop furnished rental-ready properties**
   - Furnished units command a **100% rent premium** — build furnishing packages into delivery to capture this premium for end-investor buyers
   - Position projects with "investment-grade" messaging showing projected rental yields

3. **Pursue North Coast with caution**
   - While prices are the highest (96K–119K EGP/sqm), the market is seasonal and yields are misleading at face value
   - Focus on **Ras Al Hekma** (93K/sqm, 412 listings) as a growth area rather than saturated Sidi Abdel Rahman (119K/sqm)

4. **Fill the supply gap in Giza's rental market**
   - Giza has 29.5% of sale listings but only 18.1% of rental listings — there's a **structural mismatch** suggesting many Giza properties are bought as investments but not yet entering the rental pool
   - Develop rental-optimized projects in Sheikh Zayed and 6 October targeting the under-served rental demand

---

### 📈 For Real Estate Investors

5. **Cairo apartments offer the best risk-adjusted rental yield**
   - Cairo's 7.60% gross yield, combined with the deepest tenant pool (80% of all rentals), provides the most reliable income stream
   - Target **New Cairo City** — largest market, 64K/sqm, and strong rental demand from corporate tenants and expats

6. **Invest in furnished properties for maximum rental income**
   - A 35,000 EGP/mo unfurnished unit can command 70,000 EGP/mo furnished — a 100% uplift
   - Furnishing cost for a 160 sqm apartment (~200K–400K EGP) pays for itself in 3–6 months of incremental rent

7. **Avoid Alexandria as a pure yield play**
   - At 4.47% gross yield and only 146 rental listings, Alexandria offers both the lowest returns and the thinnest market
   - If investing there, focus on capital appreciation in emerging compounds rather than rental income

8. **Consider Hurghada for diversification**
   - 1,282 sale listings (deep enough market for liquidity), 74,400 EGP/sqm, and median prices of just 6.92M EGP
   - Tourism-driven demand provides a natural tenant base, and Red Sea yields (8.06%) outperform Cairo

---

### 🏢 For Real Estate Agencies & Brokers

9. **Specialize by segment — the market is bifurcated**
   - The affordable segment (apartments < 5.7M) requires volume-based strategy (high turnover, lower commissions, digital marketing)
   - The premium segment (villas > 18M) requires relationship-based strategy (private viewings, exclusive listings, concierge service)
   - Trying to serve both with the same approach will underperform

10. **Double down on New Cairo City**
    - With 6,426 listings (35% of all sales), this is where transaction volume lives — **any brokerage not deeply penetrated in New Cairo is leaving money on the table**
    - Build compound-level expertise: know the specific developments, delivery timelines, and resale performance

11. **Build a rental management division**
    - 56.8% of rentals are fully furnished → tenants want turnkey solutions
    - Opportunity to offer end-to-end: tenant sourcing, furnishing procurement, property management, maintenance
    - With 18,917 rental listings and limited professional management, this is a blue-ocean service

---

### 🏛️ For Policymakers & Urban Planners

12. **Address the geographic imbalance**
    - 98.4% of rental supply is in Greater Cairo — this extreme concentration drives up rents and limits workforce mobility
    - Incentivize rental development in Alexandria, Suez, and secondary cities through tax benefits or reduced licensing fees

13. **Support affordable housing below the current floor**
    - The cheapest quartile starts at 5.70M EGP — far above what average Egyptian households can afford
    - The online-listed formal market effectively excludes the bottom 70% of income earners
    - Consider subsidized mortgage programs targeting the 1–3M EGP price band (nearly absent in this dataset)

14. **Regulate the North Coast bubble carefully**
    - SQM prices 2× Cairo's suggest significant speculative premium
    - Monitor for over-leveraged developers and ensure delivery commitments are met before further approvals
